Model Information

  • Model: XGBoost Classifier
  • Task: Binary classification (Fire / Not Fire)
  • Dataset: Algerian Forest Fires (UCI)
  • Samples: 244 (Bejaia & Sidi Bel-Abbes, 2012)

About the Project

Wildfire risk is rising with changing climate and weather. Early, data-driven prediction helps authorities prepare before ignition. This project applies machine learning to weather and FWI measurements (SDG 13 – Climate Action, SDG 15 – Life on Land).
